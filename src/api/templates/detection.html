Confidence bands

Fired rules divided by rules that could be evaluated in that window:

  • below {{ detection.confidence_bands.medium_at }} gives low
  • {{ detection.confidence_bands.medium_at }} to {{ detection.confidence_bands.high_at }} gives medium
  • {{ detection.confidence_bands.high_at }} and above gives high

The denominator is what the window could actually measure, not a fixed seven. Dividing by seven on a conn.log would cap every possible confidence at three sevenths and report "low" for total agreement among everything the telemetry could say.
